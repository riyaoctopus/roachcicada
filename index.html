About The Project

A Rare Breed of Luxury

Brought to you by Roach Lifescapes—the esteemed visionaries behind RBD Stillwaters and RBD Meadows—Roach Cicada represents the pinnacle of modern architectural elegance in Junnasandra.

Spanning an intimate ~2.64 acres, this G+10 tower is intentionally low-density, housing merely 124 families. Our philosophy prioritizes your personal space, boasting sprawling, Vastu-compliant layouts characterized by abundance of natural light, superior ventilation, and exceptionally generous balconies.
