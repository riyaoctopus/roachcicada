## What this means for a buyer

- The amenity count and spread (outdoor + two separate rooftop terrace zones + indoor clubhouse) is unusually extensive for a 124-unit, 2.65-acre project — this is a deliberate differentiator the developer is leaning on given the otherwise modest unit count.
- A **co-working lounge** and **pickleball court** are both relatively new-generation amenities (not standard in older Bangalore developments) and may be worth highlighting to buyers who specifically ask about work-from-home or newer recreational trends.
- Because amenities split across ground floor and two separate rooftop terraces (Tower 1&2 vs Tower 3), a buyer should be told **which amenities are on their own tower's terrace** versus which require walking to a different tower or down to ground level.
