# Sample Flat Photos, Additional Renders, and Video — Roach Cicada

This file documents media assets beyond the official brochure and website — real photographs of a furnished sample/show flat, additional exterior renders, and two videos. These are high-value for buyers because the sample flat photos show **actual finishes and fixtures**, not just 3D renders.

## Sample flat photos (real photography)

Folder: `assets/sample-flat-photos/` — 55 photographs, `assets/sample-flat-photos/sample-flat-01.jpeg` through `assets/sample-flat-photos/sample-flat-55.jpeg`, numbered in the chronological order they were shared by the developer.

These are real photographs of a furnished, fully finished sample/show apartment (not CGI renders), showing the actual quality of construction, fittings, and interior design a buyer can expect. Confirmed room-by-room, with file paths so specific rooms can be pulled up directly:

- **Master bedroom / wardrobe** — `assets/sample-flat-photos/sample-flat-01.jpeg` shows a floor-to-ceiling mirrored/glass-panel wardrobe with an upholstered bench and floating desk. `assets/sample-flat-photos/sample-flat-02.jpeg` shows the master bedroom itself: a four-poster canopy bed, upholstered headboard, pendant lighting, and sheer curtains over a garden-facing window.
- **Second bedroom** — `assets/sample-flat-photos/sample-flat-17.jpeg` (window seating, pendant light, nightstand, garden view) and `assets/sample-flat-photos/sample-flat-40.jpeg` (wall-mounted AC unit, roller blind, minimalist floating desk/headboard combination).
- **En-suite / common bathroom** — `assets/sample-flat-photos/sample-flat-16.jpeg`: rain shower head, wall-hung WC with concealed cistern panel, wood-slat false ceiling, stone-tile wall cladding, and a window (natural ventilation).
- **Kids' bedroom** — `assets/sample-flat-photos/sample-flat-10.jpeg` shows a soft pastel-toned wardrobe with round wood pulls and open cubbies holding stuffed toys, next to a study desk. `assets/sample-flat-photos/sample-flat-49.jpeg` shows a bunk bed with a red-painted steel ladder and safety rail against a large illustrated forest/animal wall mural.
- **Kitchen** — `assets/sample-flat-photos/sample-flat-07.jpeg`: a two-door frost-free refrigerator, quartz/stone island countertop with bar seating, wood-finish overhead and base cabinetry, and pendant lighting.
- **Living and dining areas** — two distinct furniture layout variants are shown (suggesting either two different sample units or two staging options): `assets/sample-flat-photos/sample-flat-13.jpeg` shows an open sofa arrangement facing floor-to-ceiling glazed doors with a garden view; `assets/sample-flat-photos/sample-flat-31.jpeg` shows a wall-mounted-TV console layout with accent armchairs. `assets/sample-flat-photos/sample-flat-25.jpeg` and `assets/sample-flat-photos/sample-flat-38.jpeg` show a round dining table with a full place-setting adjoining an open-plan kitchen.
- **Foyer / study nook** — `assets/sample-flat-photos/sample-flat-20.jpeg`: a floating desk with pendant strip lighting near a bedroom entrance.

The remaining files (`sample-flat-03.jpeg` through `sample-flat-55.jpeg`, excluding the ones named above) are additional angles and detail shots from the same walkthrough — same furnished sample apartment, covering the same set of rooms from different viewpoints.

**What this tells a buyer:** the finishes shown (stone countertops, branded appliances, rain showers, wood-slat ceilings, mirrored wardrobes) are consistent with the "luxury boutique" positioning described in [01-project-overview.md](01-project-overview.md). Note that sample flats in Indian residential projects are typically furnished for staging purposes — furniture, decor, and soft furnishings shown are **not included** in the unit price (see [03-pricing-and-payment-plan.md](03-pricing-and-payment-plan.md) for what is and isn't included); only fixed fittings (wardrobes, kitchen counters, sanitaryware, flooring) reflect what's actually delivered, and even those should be confirmed against the final specification sheet at booking.
