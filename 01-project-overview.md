# Project Overview — Roach Cicada

## What it is

Roach Cicada is a newly launched (prelaunch, bookings open) residential apartment project by **Roach Lifescapes LLP** (formerly RBD Shelters LLP), located in Junnasandra, just off Sarjapur Road in East Bangalore, directly opposite the old Wipro corporate campus. It is positioned as a low-density, boutique luxury residential development — 124 apartments spread across three towers on a 2.65-acre parcel, rather than a high-volume mega-project.

## Structure and scale

- **Towers:** 3 towers, labelled **A, B, and C** in the developer's key plans. Towers A and B share one rooftop terrace/landscape zone ("Tower 1 & 2"); Tower C has its own separate rooftop terrace zone ("Tower 3"). (Note: one third-party RERA aggregator describes the filing as "2 towers" — see [08-rera-legal-and-fine-print.md](08-rera-legal-and-fine-print.md) for this discrepancy.)
- **Height:** B+G+10 — basement(s) plus ground floor plus 10 upper floors.
- **Parking:** 2-level basement parking (dedicated basement parking for residents).
- **Total units:** 124 apartments across all towers.
- **Land area:** 2.65 acres (developer marketing figure, most recent WhatsApp sales broadcast). Other sources cite 2.64 acres or 3.14–3.15 acres — see the RERA/legal file for why these differ.
- **Open space:** Developer materials describe roughly 75% open/green space across the site, with the towers occupying a comparatively small footprint and the balance given to landscaped gardens, lawns, courts, and pathways (see the master site plan in `assets/brochure_pages/06-master-site-plan.png`).
- **Floor-to-floor height:** approximately 3.15 m (per developer's website specification).
- **Vastu:** Marketed as Vastu-compliant / Vastu-aligned layouts.

## Approvals and status

- **BBMP approved** (Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike — the local municipal planning authority).
- **RERA approved/registered** — Registration No. `PRM/KA/RERA/1251/446/PR/051225/008318`, approved 5 December 2025.
- **Launch date:** Project officially launched 1 October 2025.
- **Sales status (as of documentation date, August 2026):** Bookings open, prelaunch pricing offer active — prelaunch base rate of ₹13,200 per sq.ft.

## Design and positioning

- Marketed as a **boutique / low-density luxury enclave** — the emphasis in developer materials is on fewer homes per acre, larger balconies, and generous open space rather than density.
- Architecture: modern high-rise towers with curved, oversized balconies, wood-tone banding at the roofline, and landscaped ground-floor setbacks. See exterior renders in `assets/web_images/exterior-render-cam03-day.webp`, `assets/web_images/exterior-render-cam05-day.webp`, `assets/web_images/exterior-render-cam07-dusk.webp`, `assets/web_images/exterior-render-cam14-day.webp`, `assets/web_images/exterior-render-cam15-pool-day.webp` and `assets/brochure_pages/03-exterior-render-wide.png`, `assets/brochure_pages/04-exterior-render-balconies.png`, `assets/brochure_pages/05-exterior-render-low-angle.png`.
- The clubhouse and a large share of amenities are distributed between ground-level outdoor zones and dedicated rooftop terrace landscaping on top of the towers (see [06-master-plan-and-landscape.md](06-master-plan-and-landscape.md)).
- Unit interiors emphasize sunlit rooms, oversized balconies, and (on select higher floors of some unit types) **private terraces** in addition to standard balconies.

## Possession timeline

- **Marketed possession date:** December 2029.
- **RERA-filed completion date:** 5 August 2030 (the regulator-facing completion date typically carries a buffer beyond the marketed possession date — this is normal for RERA filings in India, but buyers should be aware the two dates differ by roughly 8 months).

Junnasandra Main Road, off Sarjapur Road, Varthur Hobli, Bengaluru East — approximately 600 metres from the old Wipro corporate campus. Full connectivity detail is in [04-location-and-connectivity.md](04-location-and-connectivity.md).
