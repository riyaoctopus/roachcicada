# Buyer FAQ — Roach Cicada

Direct question-and-answer pairs, written for retrieval. Each answer is self-contained so it can be pulled independently. Cross-references point to the detailed topic files for anything needing more depth.

### What is Roach Cicada?
Roach Cicada is a residential apartment project by Roach Lifescapes LLP (formerly RBD Shelters LLP), located in Junnasandra, off Sarjapur Road, East Bangalore, directly opposite the old Wipro corporate campus. It has 124 apartments across 3 towers on a 2.65-acre site, offered in 3 BHK, 3.5 BHK, and 4.5 BHK configurations. See [01-project-overview.md](01-project-overview.md).

### Where exactly is it located?
Junnasandra Main Road, off Sarjapur Road, Varthur Hobli, Bengaluru East, Karnataka 560035 — about 600 metres from the old Wipro campus. See [04-location-and-connectivity.md](04-location-and-connectivity.md) for full distance tables to IT parks, hospitals, schools, and retail.

### Who is the developer?
Roach Lifescapes LLP, previously known as RBD Shelters LLP (same legal entity, rebranded). Founded 1994, based in Bangalore, corporate office in Indiranagar. Prior projects include RBD Stillwaters, RBD Meadows, and the LEED-certified Radisson Blu Hotel. See [07-developer-profile.md](07-developer-profile.md).

### What unit types are available?
3 BHK (2,100–2,230 sq.ft SBA), 3.5 BHK — listed as "2.5 & 3.5 BHK" (1,839–2,400 sq.ft SBA), and 4.5 BHK (2,665–2,954 sq.ft SBA). Exact size depends on which tower/stack and which floor. See [02-unit-types-and-floor-plans.md](02-unit-types-and-floor-plans.md).

### What is the price?
Prelaunch base rate is ₹13,200 per sq.ft. All-in grand total (including GST, club house, amenities, DG, BESCOM, one car park, maintenance, and corpus fund) is approximately **₹3.19 Cr for a 3 BHK, ₹3.49 Cr for a 3.5 BHK, and ₹4.29 Cr for a 4.5 BHK**. Stamp duty, registration, ₹50,000 legal charges, Khata transfer, and BWSSB connection charges are extra. Full breakdown in [03-pricing-and-payment-plan.md](03-pricing-and-payment-plan.md).

### Is the price negotiable or will it increase?
This is an active prelaunch offer; historical data shows prices have already moved upward from an earlier ₹2.75 Cr starting figure to the current ₹13,200/sq.ft base rate. Prices are explicitly stated to be valid "for a limited period" and "subject to change" per the official cost sheet. Buyers should confirm current pricing directly with the sales team, as figures in this documentation reflect August 2026. See [08-rera-legal-and-fine-print.md](08-rera-legal-and-fine-print.md).

### What is the payment schedule?
A construction-linked plan: 9.90% on booking, 10.10% within 45 days of booking, then 10% installments at foundation/plinth/1st floor slab/3rd floor slab/5th floor slab, dropping to 5% installments at 7th floor slab through registration. Full milestone table in [03-pricing-and-payment-plan.md](03-pricing-and-payment-plan.md).

### What is included in the quoted price, and what's extra?
Included: basic price, club house, general amenities, DG charges, BESCOM (electricity) connection, one car park slot, GST (5%), first-cycle maintenance, and corpus fund. **Not included**: stamp duty, registration cost, ₹50,000 legal charges (payable at registration), Khata transfer charges, BWSSB water connection charges (amount/timing not yet determined by the developer), and betterment charges. See [03-pricing-and-payment-plan.md](03-pricing-and-payment-plan.md).

### How many towers and floors does the project have?
3 towers (A, B, C), each B+G+10 (basement + ground + 10 upper floors), with 2 levels of basement parking. Towers A and B share one rooftop terrace zone; Tower C has its own. See [01-project-overview.md](01-project-overview.md) and [06-master-plan-and-landscape.md](06-master-plan-and-landscape.md).

### How many total units are there?
124 apartments across all 3 towers.

### What is the possession date?
Marketed possession is December 2029. The RERA-filed completion date is 5 August 2030 (regulatory filings typically include extra buffer beyond the marketed date). See [01-project-overview.md](01-project-overview.md) and [08-rera-legal-and-fine-print.md](08-rera-legal-and-fine-print.md).

Yes. RERA registration number `PRM/KA/RERA/1251/446/PR/051225/008318`, approved 5 December 2025. The registered promoter of record is RBD Shelters LLP (the legal name behind the Roach Lifescapes brand). Buyers should verify the live certificate on the Karnataka RERA portal before paying. See [08-rera-legal-and-fine-print.md](08-rera-legal-and-fine-print.md).

### Is the project BBMP approved?
Yes, BBMP (municipal) approval is in place per developer materials.

### What amenities does the project offer?
50+ amenities across three zones: **outdoor** (swimming pool, kids' pool, box cricket, skate park, basketball practice court, children's play area, outdoor gym, pet corner, jogging track, event lawn, and more), **rooftop terrace** (pickleball court, yoga/meditation lawn, barbecue lawn, sunset deck, senior citizens' court, reflexology path, arts & crafts zone, and more — split across two separate terrace zones for Tower 1&2 vs Tower 3), and **indoor clubhouse** (gym with sauna, badminton court, table tennis, billiards, foosball, carrom, multipurpose hall, AV room, guest rooms, co-working lounge). Full list in [05-amenities.md](05-amenities.md).

### Does every unit get a private terrace?
No. Private terraces appear only on specific floors of specific unit stacks (typically one floor per stack, such as the 2nd floor or the 4th/7th/10th floor group depending on the stack). Most units have balconies, not private terraces. Ask which specific unit numbers include this feature. See [02-unit-types-and-floor-plans.md](02-unit-types-and-floor-plans.md).

### What is the difference between super built-up area (SBA) and RERA carpet area?
SBA is the marketed/quoted size and includes common areas, walls, and shared spaces proportionally. RERA carpet area is the actual usable floor area inside the unit's walls — the legally defined figure. For Roach Cicada, carpet area runs roughly 62–65% of the quoted SBA across all unit types. See [02-unit-types-and-floor-plans.md](02-unit-types-and-floor-plans.md).

### Does the 3 BHK have a maid's room?
No — the standard 3 BHK layout does not include a dedicated maid's room. The 3.5 BHK and 4.5 BHK types both include one (with an attached toilet).

### Is the project Vastu-compliant?
Yes, marketed as Vastu-aligned/Vastu-compliant across unit layouts.

### How much parking is provided?
2 levels of basement parking. The base cost sheet price includes one car park slot ("Car Park 1"); a second slot ("Car Park 2") is a separate line item on the cost sheet (price not filled in the current sheet — confirm availability and cost with sales).

### What is the maintenance cost?
Calculated at ₹6 per sq.ft plus GST, charged on the unit's saleable area. For example, a 3 BHK works out to about ₹1,83,853 as an initial maintenance charge component within the grand total. See [03-pricing-and-payment-plan.md](03-pricing-and-payment-plan.md).

### Is there a corpus fund charge?
Yes, a flat ₹1,00,000 corpus fund charge (no GST applies to this) is included in the grand total for every unit type.

### How close is this to Wipro / the IT corridor?
About 600 metres from the old Wipro campus, with several other tech parks (Vaishnavi Tech Park, RGA Tech Park, new Wipro campus, Ecospace, Ecoworld) within 3–5 km. Electronics City is about 11 km away. Full list in [04-location-and-connectivity.md](04-location-and-connectivity.md).

### Is there metro access nearby?
Two metro stations are described as "upcoming" (not yet operational as of this documentation): a Sarjapur Road metro station about 1 km away and a Bellandur metro station about 2.5 km away. Buyers should treat this as a future, unconfirmed-timeline benefit rather than current infrastructure.

### What schools are nearby?
Closest options include St. Peters School (1.5 km), National Public School (2 km), Orchids International School (2.5 km), and Asia Pacific World School (2.5 km). More distant options up to ~11 km include TISB, Greenwood High, Inventure Academy, and Global Indian International School. Full table in [04-location-and-connectivity.md](04-location-and-connectivity.md).

### What hospitals are nearby?
Narayana Clinic and Apollo Clinic are each about 1 km away; Motherhood Hospital (2 km), Cloud 9 Hospital (2.5 km), Manipal Hospital Sarjapur Road (3 km), Natus Hospital (3 km), and Sparsh Hospital (4 km) are all within a short drive.

### How large is the land parcel?
Marketed as 2.65 acres. Note: RERA filing records and some third-party aggregators show a larger figure (~3.14–3.15 acres), likely reflecting the total land parcel versus the net developable plot. See the discrepancy note in [08-rera-legal-and-fine-print.md](08-rera-legal-and-fine-print.md).

### How much open space does the project have?
Developer materials describe approximately 75% of the site as open/green space, with landscaped gardens, lawns, courts, and pathways surrounding the three towers.

### Has the developer built other projects?
Yes — RBD Stillwaters, RBD Meadows (both residential), and the Radisson Blu Hotel (LEED-certified, commercial/hospitality). Roach Lifescapes/RBD Shelters is credited with 4 delivered projects in Bangalore South per third-party market data. See [07-developer-profile.md](07-developer-profile.md).

### Is Roach Cicada under construction right now?
Yes. As of the RERA filing, the project had launched (1 October 2025) and was in early construction with earthwork scheduled through mid-December 2025. It is not ready-to-move; possession is targeted for December 2029.

### Why is GST charged on the price?
The 5% GST charge indicates the property is being sold under construction (pre-completion-certificate) rather than as ready-to-move inventory — GST does not apply to completed properties with an occupancy certificate. This is consistent with the multi-year construction timeline to December 2029.
