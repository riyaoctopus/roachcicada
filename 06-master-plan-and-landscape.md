# Master Plan and Landscape — Roach Cicada

## Site layout (ground level master plan)

Image: `assets/brochure_pages/06-master-site-plan.png`

The site is accessed from **Junnasandra Road**, a 12.2 m wide road, via a single entry/exit point with a security cabin arch. Internal layout runs three residential blocks (Towers A, B, C) in a roughly linear arrangement across the plot, with a central driveway/fire-tender driveway running the length of the site and a wide multipurpose lawn occupying the central spine between the towers.

## Exterior architecture

- Modern high-rise design with curved balcony fronts, wood-tone (terracotta/timber-look) banding at rooflines and select balcony undersides, and a landscaped street-facing boundary wall with plantings.

## What this means for a buyer

- A buyer in **Tower C** has an independent rooftop amenity zone from buyers in **Towers A/B** — the specific amenity mix per terrace differs slightly (e.g., "infinity walking" and "lawn with informal seating" appear only on the Tower 1&2 plan; "pebble seating" and "deck with lounge seating" appear only on the Tower 3 plan). Worth clarifying per-tower amenity access when a buyer asks "will I have access to X amenity."
- The master plan confirms a single vehicular entry/exit, which is standard for gated communities and relevant to buyers asking about site security and traffic flow.
