# Pricing and Payment Plan — Roach Cicada

Source: official "Roach Cicada — Group Cost Sheet" (Roach Lifescapes, shared by the sales team) and the official WhatsApp sales broadcast. These are the most authoritative pricing sources available (more authoritative than third-party listing portals, which show slightly different rounded figures — see [08-rera-legal-and-fine-print.md](08-rera-legal-and-fine-print.md)).

## Prelaunch offer

**Base rate: ₹13,200 per sq.ft** — flat across all three unit types (3 BHK, 3.5 BHK, 4.5 BHK), applied to the minimum saleable area for each type. No floor-rise loading (₹25/floor is the stated rate but shows as ₹0 in the current prelaunch offer) and no direction loading (East/North facing premium) are currently being applied — both are called out as line items that could apply outside the prelaunch window.

## Fine print / cost-sheet notes

These are direct notes from the official cost sheet and matter for any buyer asking "is there anything extra I need to pay":

- All taxes are at rates prevailing at time of payment — GST and other charges are subject to change.
- E-stamp paper amount is payable at the time of the Sale Deed.
- **Stamp duty and registration cost are extra**, applicable at the date of registration, and borne by the purchaser (not included in the grand total above).
- **Legal charges of ₹50,000** must be paid by the purchaser at the time of registration.
- Premium facing charges and floor-rise charges may apply depending on the specific unit's location within the project (not currently charged during the prelaunch offer).
- The cost sheet is not final — a final cost sheet is issued by the CRM and Accounts team at the time of booking confirmation.
- **Khata transfer charges are not included** in the above cost.
- **BWSSB (water supply board) connection charges are not currently being collected.** According to the developer, this connection is dependent on government/BWSSB timelines and the future Apartment Owners' Association, and the developer has not yet been able to assess a timeframe — so this is a real future cost with an unknown amount and timing.
- **Betterment charges are not collected** in the above cost sheet and will be payable separately by the purchaser when levied.
- Apartment naming convention: Tower + Floor + Unit Number (e.g., a unit would be identified as something like "A-703").
- **This cost sheet is not a legal offering and does not confirm unit availability** — it is an indicative pricing document only.
- These prices are valid for a limited period (prelaunch window) and all rates are subject to change.

## What a buyer should take away

- The **effective all-in price per sq.ft** (grand total ÷ SBA) works out to roughly ₹14,700–₹14,900/sq.ft depending on unit type — higher than the ₹13,200 headline "base rate" once GST, amenities charges, DG, BESCOM, car park, maintenance, and corpus fund are added.
- Registration-related costs (stamp duty, registration fee, ₹50,000 legal charges, and eventual Khata transfer + BWSSB connection charges) sit **on top of** the grand total and are not fixed numbers yet.
- The 5% GST rate implies this is being sold as an under-construction property without completion certificate (GST does not apply to ready-to-move properties with an occupancy certificate) — consistent with the December 2029 / August 2030 possession timeline.
