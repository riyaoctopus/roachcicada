The site sits opposite the old Wipro corporate campus, just off the Sarjapur Road / Outer Ring Road junction area. ## What this means for a buyer

- The single strongest locational selling point is **proximity to the old Wipro campus (600 m)** and several other IT parks (Vaishnavi Tech Park, RGA Tech Park, new Wipro campus, Ecospace, Ecoworld) all within a 5 km radius — this is a project aimed squarely at IT-sector buyers working in the Sarjapur Road / Outer Ring Road corridor.
- Two metro stations (Sarjapur Road and Bellandur) are described as "upcoming" — meaning **not yet operational**. A buyer relying on metro connectivity should treat this as a future benefit, not a current one, and should independently verify construction status/timeline before treating it as a firm commitment.
- The location is well served by hospitals and schools within a 1–5 km radius, which is typically a priority for family buyers.
