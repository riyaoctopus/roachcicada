# Developer Profile — Roach Lifescapes

## Identity

- **Current brand name:** Roach Lifescapes LLP
- **Prior/legal name:** RBD Shelters LLP (the entity registered as the RERA promoter for this project is recorded as "RBD Shelters LLP" — see [08-rera-legal-and-fine-print.md](08-rera-legal-and-fine-print.md)). The developer's own sales materials explicitly state "Roach Lifescapes LLP (formerly RBD Shelters)," confirming this is a rebrand of the same legal entity rather than a different company.
- **Structure:** Limited Liability Partnership, established 1994, with 4 partners per the RERA filing.
- **Corporate office:** Tiara 682, Indiranagar, Bangalore.
- **Focus:** Mid-to-premium residential and plotted developments in Bangalore, described in the developer's own materials as a "boutique real estate brand."

## Track record

Roach Lifescapes / RBD Shelters is credited (across the developer's own site and third-party research) with:
- **RBD Stillwaters** — a prior residential project.
- **RBD Meadows** — a prior residential project.
- **Radisson Blu Hotel** — a LEED-certified, award-winning hospitality project, indicating the developer also has commercial/hospitality delivery experience, not just residential.
- Described by third-party market data as one of the known real estate brands in Bangalore South, with **4 projects delivered** to date.

## Positioning

Marketing materials emphasize "over 30 years of residential development experience" (consistent with the 1994 founding date) and a stated design philosophy of "practical layouts, open spaces, greenery" — this framing is echoed in Roach Cicada's own low-density, high-open-space positioning (see [01-project-overview.md](01-project-overview.md)).

## What this means for a buyer

- This is **not** a first-time or unknown developer — the 1994 founding date and a completed hotel + two prior residential projects give some track record to point to, though the buyer should independently verify possession/delivery history of RBD Stillwaters and RBD Meadows (e.g., were they delivered on time, any consumer complaints) since that detail was not available in the sources used for this documentation.
- The **name change from RBD Shelters to Roach Lifescapes** is worth being upfront about if a buyer cross-checks the RERA certificate and sees "RBD Shelters LLP" instead of "Roach Lifescapes" — this is the same company, not a discrepancy to be concerned about, but it can look confusing without this context.
