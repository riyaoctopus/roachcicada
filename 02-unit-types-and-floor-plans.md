# Unit Types and Floor Plans — Roach Cicada

All figures below are transcribed directly from the developer's official floor-plan brochure ("Apartment Book Final"). SBA = Super Built-up Area. RERA Carpet = the RERA-defined carpet area (usable area), which is the legally binding area figure under Indian real estate law.

There are 3 towers (A, B, C). Each unit "type" (3 BHK, 3.5 BHK, 4.5 BHK) has several near-identical layout variants depending on which tower and which stack (position within the tower) the unit sits in — the room count and general layout are the same, but exact dimensions and SBA shift by a few dozen square feet between variants. Every unit also varies slightly by floor: ground-floor units get a "sit-out" instead of a balcony (smaller SBA), one specific floor in the stack (typically 2nd or 5th/8th) gets a bonus **private terrace**, and standard floors get a straightforward balcony layout.

## Notes for buyers

- **SBA vs RERA carpet:** the gap between super built-up area and RERA carpet area is roughly 35–38% across all unit types (i.e., carpet area is about 62–65% of the quoted SBA). This is the figure to use when comparing "actual usable space" against other projects.
- **Private terraces** are not universal — they appear on specific floors of specific stacks only. A buyer wanting a private terrace should ask which exact unit numbers carry it.
- **Ground floor units** are meaningfully smaller in SBA than upper floors of the same nominal type (because they get a sit-out instead of a full balcony), but RERA carpet area for ground floor 3.5 BHK units is also smaller (1,176 sq.ft vs 1,452–1,453 sq.ft on upper floors) — this is a real reduction in indoor space, not just a balcony difference.
- All units are Vastu-aligned per developer marketing (see [01-project-overview.md](01-project-overview.md)).
